# HR-Analytics-Project-Atliq-Technologies
AtliQ Technologies' HR Generalist wanted to gain insights into employee attendance patterns using three months of historical attendance data. The objective was to analyze employee presence, identify work-from-home (WFH) and work-from-office (WFO) patterns, and understand trends in sick leave. These insights could help HR identify potential workforce planning concerns and support data-driven HR decisions.

# Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end HR analytics workflow focused on employee attendance patterns. The dashboard analyzes attendance (presence %), work-from-home (WFH) %, and sick leave (SL) trends across employees, dates, and days of the week. The goal is to help HR teams identify attendance patterns, potential operational risks, and workforce planning opportunities through interactive reporting.
The solution was developed using Power BI and Excel, with an emphasis on effective data modeling, accurate DAX measures, and clear, KPI-driven visual design.

## What This Dashboard Covers

- **Employee-level attendance tracking (Present, Work From Home, and Sick Leave)**
- **Time-series analysis of attendance trends**
- **Day-of-week attendance patterns**
- **Summary KPIs for management reporting**
- **Detailed tables for operational review**

## Key Insights & Capabilities

- **Built an interactive HR attendance analytics dashboard using Power BI and Excel, transforming raw attendance logs into structured, analysis-ready data.**
- **Developed a data model and DAX measures to track attendance (presence rate), WFH rate, and sick leave (SL) rate over time.**
- **Created management-friendly KPIs and trend visualizations to highlight attendance fluctuations, weekday patterns, and potential workforce planning concerns.**
- **Enabled faster HR analysis by consolidating multiple attendance views into a single, interactive reporting layer.**


## Tools & Skills Used

-**Power BI: Data modeling, DAX measures, interactive dashboards, and data visualization**

-**Excel: Data preparation, cleaning, and validation**

-**Data Analytics: Trend analysis and KPI development**

-**Business Intelligence: Reporting and dashboard design**
